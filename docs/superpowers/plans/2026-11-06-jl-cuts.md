# J- and L-Cuts — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A shot's own sound can start before its picture (a J-cut: you hear the next scene first) or carry on under the next shot (an L-cut: a laugh rings over the cut), with the neighbouring shot's sound crossfading so two voices never talk over each other at full level.

**Architecture:** Two optional item fields, `leadIn` and `tailOut`. A pure module works out each item's usable lead and tail (clamped by the source the clip has and by the neighbouring shots) and the crossfades. Chunks keep each item's own sound, with fades applied where a neighbour's lead or tail overlaps it; the lead and tail pieces render as a separate "spill" track, decoded from the sources and placed with `adelay`, mixed into the speech bus before normalising. `speechWords` includes the spilled words, so captions and slots see them; `check` warns about shortened or ineffective values and about voice-over lines under spilled sound.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, zod 4, vitest, FFmpeg (`atrim`, `afade` with `curve=qsin`, `adelay`, `amix`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-editing-tools-and-vlog-design.md`, part 4.

## Global Constraints
- Episodes without `leadIn`/`tailOut`: byte-identical FFmpeg arguments, captions, slots and `check` output. Both fields `.optional()` on clip items (`number().min(0).max(3)`); card items don't have them.
- They only work on clip items with `audio: "keep"` at `speed: 1` whose clip has sound. Elsewhere `check` warns (exact) `item <id> has leadIn/tailOut but doesn't keep its own sound (audio "keep" at speed 1) — it does nothing` and the render ignores them.
- Usable lead = min(`leadIn`, the clip's source before `src.in`, the previous item's output length); usable tail = min(`tailOut`, the clip's source after `src.out` (its audio duration), the next item's output length). A lead on the first item or a tail on the last item has nothing to overlap: usable 0. `check` warns (exact) when a value is shortened: `item <id>'s leadIn of <x> s is shortened to <y> s — <reason>` (same for `tailOut`), reason one of `<clip> has only <y> s of sound before the shot`, `<clip> has only <y> s of sound after the shot`, `the shot before it is only <y> s long`, `the shot after it is only <y> s long`, `it's the first shot`, `it's the last shot`.
- Crossfade (equal power, `curve=qsin`): over a lead's window `[start − lead, start]` the previous item's own sound fades out while the lead fades in; over a tail's window `[end, end + tail]` the next item's own sound fades in while the tail fades out. A neighbour that is muted, a card, or sped up has no sound to fade — only the spill fades.
- `itemsFingerprint` doesn't include them (picture timing is unchanged), so a fitted voice-over stays fitted.
- `speechWords` includes the words inside each usable lead/tail at their output times (so captions show them and `slots` keeps narration off them). `check` warns (exact) when a fitted voice-over line overlaps spilled sound: `voice-over line <slot> plays over item <id>'s carried-over sound (<a>–<b> s) — shorten the leadIn/tailOut or move the line`.
- Holds: a held item's tail starts after its held frames (the sound carries on under the next shot, not under the freeze). A voice-over fit that splits a clip item puts `leadIn` on the first half and `tailOut` on the second half only.
- Cross-platform; commits with `git commit -s` ending `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`; nothing pushed.

## Review Focus
- **A lead and the previous item's tail meeting at the same cut** (both shots spill into each other): both crossfades apply; sound never doubles to full level from two sources at once. Pinned in Task 2.
- **A spill crossing a chunk boundary** (item 30 and 31 in different chunks): the spill track is one track for the whole episode, so it plays across the boundary; the fades on chunk audio are per item. Pinned in Task 2.
- **A clip whose sound starts late (`videoStartSec`) or whose audio runs past its picture:** the lead/tail limits use the audio's own range. Pinned in Task 1.
- **A short episode (< 3 s) with a spill:** no loudnorm NaN (the spill joins the speech bus before the existing short-episode handling). Pinned in Task 2.
- **Beat snapping or a voice-over hold changing a neighbour's length:** usable lead/tail recomputed from the final layout. Pinned in Task 1.
